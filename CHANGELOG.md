# Changelog

## 0.4.3

- **Comet Shepherd on Barren-Desert:** a Barren-Desert with no hard condition (very cold, very hot, no, thin or toxic atmosphere, irradiated, darkness) was stuck: no structure could change its type. The Comet Shepherd now works there and makes it Arid.

## 0.4.2

- **A calm Captive Sun:** a small yellow star in orbit, in place of the pulsing lamp. It still lights the planet's night side. A Captive Sun you built before turns into the star when you load the game.
- **Planet history:** in the Terraforming tab of the Intel screen, each planet with a Terraforming Control gets an entry with DTI's events and their dates. It stays when you lose the colony, and you can delete it. A save from before 0.4.2 gets an entry for each colony that has a Control.
- A console command for tests: `dti_sun`.

## 0.4.1

- **Unlock without quests** is on by default: without AoTD, every structure is unlocked from the start. With AoTD, the research still unlocks them. The setting goes back to off when the quests are in the mod. A game that already saved this setting in LunaLib keeps its value.

## 0.4.0

The first public test version.

- The Terraforming Control and 24 terraforming structures for atmosphere, temperature, light, tectonics, weather, pollution, biosphere, radiation, water, soil, marine life and climate, with planet type changes.
- The reverse projects: Atmospheric Siphon, Deep Core Extractor, Volatiles Extractor.
- Colony Development: Civic Integration, Labor Camps, Purge Detachment, Domed Cities, the City Agrav Grid, the Impact Defense Grid, Hydroponic Farms.
- 24 special items.
- DTI Projects in the Intel screen.
- With AoTD: 28 techs on their own research tab.
- LunaLib settings.
